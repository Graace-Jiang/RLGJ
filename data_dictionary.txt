Project Title: S&P 500 Dividend Policy and Industry Profitability Analysis
Date: May 2026
Contributors: Grace Jiang, Richard Li

FILE: clean_final_dataset.csv
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
This dataset integrates firm-level financial metrics for the S&P 500 with 
industry-level benchmarks.

TOTAL OBSERVATIONS: 493
TOTAL VARIABLES: 9

VARIABLES:
Ticker (String) 
- The unique stock symbol for the company (e.g., "AAPL", "MSFT"). 
- Source: yfinance / S&P 500 Ticker List.

Company (String) 
- The company name associated with the stock ticker. 
- Source: yfinance.

Industry_x (String) 
- The original industry classification provided by Yahoo Finance through yfinance. 
- Source: yfinance.

DamodaranIndustry (String) 
- The standardized industry category created through OpenRefine mapping to align Yahoo Finance industries with Damodaran industry classifications. 
- Source: OpenRefine transformation based on Yahoo Finance and Damodaran datasets.

Sector (String) 
- The broad business sector classification for the company (e.g., Technology, Healthcare). 
- Source: yfinance.

DividendYield (Float) 
- The company’s dividend yield percentage. Missing values are filled with 0 during cleaning when dividend information is unavailable. 
- Source: yfinance.

PayoutRatio (Float) 
- The proportion of earnings paid as dividends by the company. 
- Source: yfinance.

Industry_y (String) 
- The original industry category from the Damodaran dataset after merging. 
- Source: Damodaran “Margins by Industry” dataset.

NetMargin (Float) 
- The industry-level net profit margin benchmark associated with the mapped Damodaran industry category. 
- Source: Damodaran “Margins by Industry” dataset.
